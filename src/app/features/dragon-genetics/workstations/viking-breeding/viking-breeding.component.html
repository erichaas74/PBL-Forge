A settlement selects the animals it breeds from, the way an island's conditions do — except you choose. Each season you pair two dragons, keep the pups worth keeping, and release the rest. The line changes because of what you keep.

Looking at a dragon tells you its form, not the pair of alleles behind it. Two dragons that look identical can throw very different litters, so watch what a pairing actually produces rather than trusting how the parents look.

Some traits will settle down and stay. Others will keep throwing off-type pups no matter how carefully you choose. Working out which is which is the job.
