How to control your dragon

Move
Arrow pad on screen, or WASD / arrow keys. Forward charges toward the far wall; left and right swing your dragon sideways.
Leap
Hold the Leap button or Shift to run faster; winged dragons also beat their wings and get airborne. It is one beat per press, not a hover.
Claw rake
Claw button or U. The quick one: half a second start to finish and safe to throw, but it only chips away at an opponent. Every dragon has claws.
Bite
Bite button or J. Lunges the head forward; strongest when you are close and moving fast.
Wing buffet
Wing button or K. Only dragons that inherited at least one W allele can use it.
Tail sweep
Tail button or L. Whips the tail side to side — good when an opponent circles behind you.
Horn charge
Horn button or H. Runs your dragon forward and knocks the opponent flat. The heaviest hit in the game and the slowest — it can be seen coming, blocked, or rolled away from. Needs at least one H allele.
Fire breath
Fire button or F. Scorches a cone in front of you — only dragons that inherited at least one F allele can use it.
Brace
Hold the Brace button or G. Plants your dragon behind its horns: most of the damage is absorbed and nothing knocks you down, but you can barely move and cannot attack. The bar on the button is how much brace you have left — hold it too long and it collapses. Needs at least one H allele.
Roll
Roll button or Space. A wing-assisted dive to the side. There is a moment in the middle where nothing can hit you at all, so a well-timed roll beats even a horn charge — but throw it early and you spend a second going nowhere. Needs at least one W allele.
Gamepad
Plug in any controller: left stick or d-pad moves, A bites, X wing, B tail, Y fire, left trigger claws, right trigger leaps, LB braces, RB rolls.

Damage comes from real collisions and landed attacks: an attack that touches the opponent scores a hit even at low speed, and hit parts flash. The first moments after the spawn drop are a safe settling period, then the duel is live. Pause any time.

Your genotype is your moveset. Claw, bite, and tail belong to every dragon. Wings grant the buffet and the roll; horns grant the charge and the brace; fire grants the breath. A dragon with neither W nor H can still win, but it has to do it without a way to defend — which is worth noticing when you choose which hatchling to take into the arena.

Turn duel mode: no reflexes needed — pick up to three move cards, press Play turn, and watch the physics act them out. Then the Arena Warden takes its turn. Great for planning strategies or sharing one computer.
