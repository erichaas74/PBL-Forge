Arena field guideKeyboard, gamepad, traits, and tactics
Move

Use the on-screen pad, arrow keys, or WASD. Hold Shift to leap or charge.

Core strikes

Every dragon can claw, bite, and sweep its tail. Position and timing determine whether the physics registers a hit.

Inherited moves

Wings grant buffet and roll. Horns grant charge and brace. Fire inheritance grants fire breath.

Defend

Brace absorbs damage until its meter fills. A well-timed roll briefly avoids every hit, including a horn charge.

Tactical turns

Queue up to three moves and watch the same physics play them out. The Arena Warden then chooses a response.

Gamepad

Left stick moves. Face buttons attack, triggers claw and leap, left bumper braces, and right bumper rolls.
