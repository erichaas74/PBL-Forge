# Dragon Genetics Lesson 5: Meiosis and Dragon Eggs

Students choose from two female and two male starter dragons, investigate meiosis in either parent, and combine one selected egg cell with one selected sperm cell.

- **Dragon Arena:** Ember and Moss are the female starters; Tide and Quartz are the male starters.
- **Mini Dragon Show:** Biscuit and Cinder are the female starters; Pepper and Nimbus are the male starters. The resulting egg records mini-dragon Wings, Back Scales, Coat Pattern, and Horns allele pairs.

## Evidence sequence

- A diploid parent cell begins with homologous chromosome pairs.
- Chromosomes replicate.
- Homologs pair and may exchange matching segments during crossing over.
- Homologous chromosomes separate during meiosis I.
- Sister chromatids separate during meiosis II.
- Four genetically varied haploid cells result.
- One egg cell and one sperm cell combine, restoring chromosome pairs in a fertilized dragon egg.
- Students can inspect the alleles in the egg before revealing the offspring.

The meiosis control supports replaying the run, slowing crossing over, inspecting recombinant chromosomes, and selecting any resulting gamete. The egg and offspring records save locally without requiring Firebase.

The stages support the investigation but do not gate the learning-path navigation. Students may return to the lesson page at any time.
