# Lesson 1 — Meet the Dragons

## Required workstation decisions

1. **Scientific goal:** Distinguish inherited physical characteristics from behaviors acquired
   through training by comparing two contrasting dragons.
2. **Manipulable evidence:** Students open either specimen card, rotate and zoom both live 3D
   specimens, select any available comparison, run behavior trials, and write notebook entries.
3. **Observable consequence:** The two specimens show contrasting anatomy and produce different
   responses to the same behavior cue.
4. **Student-built record:** An editable Mystery Pair notebook records the observed difference, the
   student's genetic-or-learned classification, and their evidence statement.
5. **Shared sources:** Full-size specimens come from the Trait Evidence catalog and assembly
   renderer. Mini specimens come from the Mini Dragon founder catalog and specimen builder. The
   lesson-plan document supplies the shared goal, guide, workstation link, and reflection questions.

The investigation has no embedded question dock, correctness colors, prescribed order, or
completion gate. Shared reflection questions remain on the lesson page.
