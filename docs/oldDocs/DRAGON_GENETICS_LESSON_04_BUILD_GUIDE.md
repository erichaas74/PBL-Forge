# Dragon Genetics Lesson 4: Allele Experiments

Lesson 4 asks students to determine phenotype expression by testing allele pairs, not by being told a click sequence.

## Shared investigation

For any selected gene, students can test the three unique pairings:

1. homozygous dominant;
2. heterozygous;
3. homozygous recessive.

Each run changes the visible dragon and adds that observed genotype and phenotype to the outcome chart. After all three pairings have been observed, the completed chart states the full evidence pattern: the dominant phenotype appears with one or two dominant copies, while the recessive phenotype appears only with two recessive copies.

Arena uses full-size dragon traits. Mini Dragon Show uses actual mini dragons and complete-dominance mini traits. The lesson goal and written questions are identical.

The chart saves on the device without Firebase. It is an evidence record, not a navigation gate: students can return to the lesson or proceed at any time.
